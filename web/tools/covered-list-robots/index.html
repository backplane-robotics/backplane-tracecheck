Background

  • On 28 July 2026 the FCC added foreign-produced “advanced robotic devices” to the Covered List (reported as DA 26-786). This blocks new FCC equipment authorizations for covered equipment. Existing authorizations and devices already in use are reported as unaffected.
  • The category is reported to cover autonomous or semi-autonomous mobile ground robots (for example AMRs, humanoids, quadrupeds, and inspection, security or agricultural robots) above a weight threshold, with sensors and wireless connectivity. Fixed industrial arms, drones (listed separately), underwater vehicles, rail-bound vehicles and connected cars are reported as excluded.
  • “Foreign-produced” is reported to follow where a product is manufactured, not where the company is headquartered.
  • Conditional approvals follow a Department of War review and name specific equipment. They are reported to be tied to onshoring commitments and can be revoked.

Background points are summarized from secondary sources and have not yet been checked against the FCC originals. Notices in the same period also covered non-robot equipment (for example power inverters, routers and drones); those are not listed here.
